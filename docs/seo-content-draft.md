Known in Urdu as **salajeet**, it is one of the most searched-for natural products in Pakistan, and also one of the most often faked.

**What is shilajit?** Shilajit is a dark, sticky resin that slowly seeps from rock crevices high in the mountains during the summer heat. It forms over many years from plant matter pressed between the rocks. Local collectors gather the raw material, which is then cleaned by dissolving it in water, filtering out stones and soil, and slowly drying it back into a soft resin.

**How to recognise pure shilajit.** Pure resin is dark brown to black with a glossy surface and a strong, earthy smell. It becomes soft and sticky in warm hands and hard when cold. A small piece dissolves completely in warm water, turning it golden brown, without leaving grit or a film. Be careful with very cheap shilajit, capsules and powders, which are often mixed with other substances.

**How it is traditionally taken.** A small, pea-sized portion (about 300 to 500 mg) is dissolved in a cup of warm water, milk or herbal tea, usually once a day. Shilajit is a traditional product, not a medicine. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or have a medical condition, ask your doctor before use.
